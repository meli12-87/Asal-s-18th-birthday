05 — Six years, mapped out

2020 → 2026

It's strange to look at it laid out like this. Six years, start to now.

2020
A fan page, started in the middle of quarantine — and somehow, that's where I met you. I didn't think we'd end up here.
2021–2022
Getting to know you slowly, then all at once. We moved to Telegram and got closer — I watched your life update, story by story, and stayed for all of it.
2023
We went live together for the first time — my first ever livestream. Somewhere around here you became one of the most important people in my life.
2024–2025
More years, more messages, more of us — the ordinary, everyday kind of closeness that quietly became the most important thing.
2026
Six years since it started. You just turned 18. Legally an adult now — I'm still not letting go.
